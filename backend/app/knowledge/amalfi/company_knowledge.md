## Business profile
- Pizzeria Amalfi, a restaurant in Frankfurt am Main, owned and run by Vincenzo Amalfi.
- Accounting period: calendar year 2025 (1 Jan – 31 Dec 2025). Opening balance on 1 Jan 2025: €2,500.00.

## Taxes and social security
- Wage tax and solidarity surcharge: €880.00 per month on the 10th (Tax Office Frankfurt am Main).
- Social security: €2,650.00 per month on the 27th (Techniker Krankenkasse: health, pension, nursing and
  unemployment insurance).
- Income tax prepayment: €1,150.00 per quarter on 10 Mar, 10 Jun, 10 Sep and 10 Dec, based on about €30,000
  profit in the prior year.
- Trade tax prepayment: €225.00 per quarter on 15 Feb, 15 May, 15 Aug and 15 Nov.

## Fixed overhead
- Rent: €3,800.00 per month on the 1st (Main Street Real Estate GmbH).
- Energy and utilities: €650.00 per month on the 10th (City Energy & Utilities AG).

## Zurich silent partnership
- Vincenzo invests 50,000.00 CHF as a silent partner in his brother Luigi Amalfi's new restaurant in Zurich,
  Switzerland.
- Paid in 12 monthly tranches of 4,166.67 CHF on the 15th, converted to euros at the day's rate (about
  €4,325–€4,375 per tranche). All 12 tranches were paid in 2025.
- Because the payments are in Swiss francs, their euro cost changes with the exchange rate (currency risk).

## Revenue channels
- Debit-card takings: settled daily into the business account.
- Credit-card takings: paid out twice a week via Commerzbank Global Pay.
- Cash: minimal; customers mostly pay by card.

## Suppliers
- Fattoria La Vino GmbH: weekly payments for Italian wines and drinks.
- Asimex Wholesale Ltd: frequent invoice payments for food and imported specialty ingredients.
- Central Wholesale Market: fresh produce bought most mornings, paid on site by Girocard.

## 2025 story: June shutdown and recovery
- June shutdown: the pizzeria closed for 3 weeks in June for the summer holiday, with no revenue at all.
- Fixed costs continued (rent, payroll, taxes, social security and the Zurich tranche), so the account went
  into a temporary overdraft that lasted into early July.
- Recovery: strong card takings in Q3 and Q4 absorbed the deficit, and the year closed with a surplus.
